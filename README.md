A 2D mobile underwater exploration game developed in Unity where diver dives in underwater enviroment and progress through increasing levels of difficulty, manage oxygen, 
avoid sea creatures, collect boosters, reach checkpoints, and save progress. It integrates progress saving, leaderboard, authentication using Node.js and SQLite.

player should: 
- Players can register, log in, and resume progress from the last saved state.
- Navigate a diver through four levels: Easy, Medium, Hard, and Very Hard.
- Each level contains checkpoints, boosters, and sea creatures.
- The diver loses oxygen over time and when hit by creatures.
- Boosters restore oxygen and help survival.
- After reaching all checkpoints, the player progresses to the next level.
- Scores are calculated based on oxygen and time.
- Final scores are uploaded and shown in a Leaderboard.
